Modern Scandinavian Tiny Home

San Diego, California
$85,000
1 / 8

Description

Experience the perfect blend of Scandinavian minimalism and modern functionality in this thoughtfully designed tiny home. Every inch has been carefully planned to maximize space while maintaining a sense of openness and tranquility.

This stunning 320 square foot home features premium finishes throughout, including hardwood floors, custom millwork, and energy-efficient appliances. The open-concept living area flows seamlessly into the kitchen, creating an inviting space perfect for both relaxation and entertaining.

The sleeping loft provides a cozy retreat with built-in storage, while the full bathroom includes a composting toilet and space-saving shower. Large windows flood the interior with natural light and offer beautiful views of the surrounding landscape.
